The questions we get asked

The courts are hostile to this.

Courts are hostile to unverified citations and undisclosed use, which is a different thing. The current practice notes in your jurisdiction are the starting material for the policy, not an obstacle to it.

We'd rather just ban it.

A ban you can't enforce is worse than a rule people follow. The audit tells you which one you actually have.

Our PI insurer will have views.

They will, and a written policy is what they will ask to see. Bring them into the scoping conversation.

Regulatory context

Relevant bodies and frameworks: State and territory law societies, Supreme Court practice notes on generative AI, Australian Institute of Conveyancers. Court and regulator guidance on generative AI changes frequently and differs by jurisdiction. Current practice notes are checked before any engagement and the page carries a review date.
