Privacy policy

Short, because we collect very little.

Reviewed September 2026. This page names fast-moving vendor detail, so it carries a review date.

What we collect

  • What you type into the assessment, if you choose to send it: your name, email, business name and your answers.
  • What you send us by email.
  • Basic, aggregate site analytics. No advertising or cross-site tracking pixels.

The assessment runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is transmitted unless you fill in the form at the end and submit it.

Why

To reply to you, to prepare a scope if you ask for one, and to send the newsletter if you opted in. Nothing else.

What we don't do

  • Sell, rent or share your details with anyone for marketing.
  • Add you to a mailing list you didn't tick a box for.
  • Put your enquiry into an AI tool. The irony would be considerable.

How long we keep it

Enquiries and assessment submissions are kept while there's an active conversation and for a reasonable period after, then deleted. Newsletter subscriptions last until you unsubscribe.

Access, correction and deletion

Email hello@ai-at-work.au and ask. We’ll confirm what we hold, correct it, or delete it.

Complaints

If we've handled your information badly, tell us and we'll fix it. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date on the page changes with it.
