What Claude is better at

Not a review. Three shapes of work where it's the one we reach for, and the ones where it isn't.

Reviewed September 2026. This page names fast-moving vendor detail, so it carries a review date.

Long input, one careful answer

Contracts, bundles, rulings, strata reports, plan sets, transcripts. Work where the input is long, the question is narrow, and a fluent wrong answer costs you something. Detail on long documents.

Sustained voice across many drafts

The fifteenth vendor report of the week should read like the first. Given examples and a held setup, this is where Claude is noticeably steadier — useful anywhere a team's output has to look like it came from one organisation.

Thinking with several moving parts

A scope with dependencies, a policy that has to satisfy three obligations at once, an argument with an order to it. Work where you want the reasoning shown so you can disagree with a step rather than just the conclusion.

Where we'd use something else

  • Twenty short jobs in an hour — the wrong shape for it.
  • Talking rather than typing, which is ChatGPT’s strength and matters enormously in the field.
  • Photo-driven tasks, where ChatGPT is generally the smoother experience.
