What we don't take on

Anything where a wrong output is a regulated harm rather than an inconvenience: clinical medicine, financial advice, tax positions as opposed to tax administration, and anything requiring domain expertise we don't hold. Credibility is the whole product here, so the boundary is published rather than discovered later.
