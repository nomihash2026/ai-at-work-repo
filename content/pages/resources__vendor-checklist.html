Vendor checklist

Increasingly the AI in your business arrives as a feature in software you already have. Send these before the demo.

The nine questions

  1. Which model provider sits behind this feature, and is that in writing?
  2. Is our content used to train or improve any model, theirs or a third party's?
  3. What's retained, for how long, and what does deletion actually delete?
  4. Where is it processed and stored, and which jurisdictions can compel access?
  5. Who can review our content, under what circumstances, and is it logged?
  6. Can the feature be turned off per user, and does that stop processing or just hide it?
  7. What happens to our data if we leave, or if you're acquired?
  8. What does the contract say about breach notification, and on what timeline?
  9. Has this been assessed against the Australian Privacy Principles, and can we see it?

Reading the answers

  • Specific, written, quick — they've done the work.
  • Reassurance without detail — they haven't, or they can't say.
  • “Enterprise-grade security” as an answer to a data-use question — not an answer.
  • A refusal to put it in writing — the most informative response available.

Their compliance is not your compliance

A vendor's assessment covers their obligations. Yours still sit with you, which is why the written answers matter more than the assurance.
