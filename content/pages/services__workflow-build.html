Done-with-you workflow build

For businesses where a classroom is the wrong shape. One job, built properly, running on real work before we finish.

Who this is for

Owner-operators and field businesses, mostly. Trades, cleaning and maintenance, small service businesses — anyone whose honest answer to “can you sit in a room for half a day” is no, and who has one specific job that's costing them money every week.

How it runs

  1. We pick the one job. Usually same-day quoting, monthly client reporting, or follow-up that never happens.
  2. We build it from your existing documents, so the output sounds like you rather than like a template.
  3. We run it on live work together, and fix what breaks. This is the part that makes it stick.
  4. We hand over with the setup, a one-page how-to, and the two follow-ups already written.

The jobs that pay for themselves fastest

  • Quote out the same day as the site visit rather than Thursday night.
  • Two follow-ups on every quote, which most businesses never send at all.
  • Job notes and variations dictated on the drive home and written up before invoicing.
  • The monthly client report that currently happens on a Sunday.

What stays with you

Certification, compliance sign-off and safety determinations don't move. Drafting speeds up; accountability doesn't shift.
