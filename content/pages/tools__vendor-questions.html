Questions to ask any AI vendor

Increasingly the AI in your business won't be a chat tool at all. It'll be a feature your existing software turned on. These are the questions for that conversation.

Reviewed September 2026. This page names fast-moving vendor detail, so it carries a review date.

The nine

  1. Which model provider sits behind this feature, and is that in writing?
  2. Is our content used to train or improve any model, theirs or a third party's?
  3. What's retained, for how long, and what does deletion actually delete?
  4. Where is it processed and stored, and which jurisdictions can compel access?
  5. Who can review our content, under what circumstances, and is it logged?
  6. Can we turn the feature off per user, and does that stop processing or just hide it?
  7. What happens to our data if we leave, or if you're acquired?
  8. What does the contract say about breach notification, and on what timeline?
  9. Has this been assessed against the Australian Privacy Principles, and can we see it?

How to use them

Send them before the demo. A vendor whose answers are specific, written and quick has done the work. A vendor whose answers arrive as reassurance rather than detail has told you something useful too.

For regulated settings

In health, NDIS and legal work these questions sit alongside your own obligations rather than replacing them. A vendor's compliance is not your compliance, and their assessment is not yours.
