Choosing between them

The honest version: if your team is already using AI and there's no written rule, the policy work comes first. Training people to go faster in a direction you haven't decided on is worse than not training them.

If the rules are fine and the problem is that nobody's any good at it yet, that's a workshop. If there's one job costing you real money every week and nobody has time to sit in a room, that's a workflow build.

The assessment makes that call from nine answers and tells you which one, including when the answer is none of them yet.
