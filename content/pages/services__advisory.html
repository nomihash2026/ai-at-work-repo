Services

Ongoing advisory

For businesses where this has become someone's job and the questions keep arriving.

What it covers

  • A vendor turns on an AI feature and someone has to decide whether to allow it.
  • The policy needs a revision because the tools changed, which they will.
  • A new hire needs the same grounding the team got.
  • Someone wants to build a setup for a new document type and wants it reviewed.
  • An auditor, insurer or funder asks a question about AI use.

What it isn't

It isn't a retainer to produce your content, and it isn't IT support. It's access to a considered answer on the questions where getting it wrong is expensive and getting it right takes half an hour of someone's attention.

Who takes it up

Mostly multi-site clinics, NDIS providers and firms above about fifteen staff — businesses where a wrong call has consequences and where the tools sit close to regulated work. Smaller businesses generally don't need it, and we'll say so.

Talk it through before you commit

Tell us what the business does and what's eating the week. If we're the wrong fit we'll say so, and usually we can point you somewhere better.
