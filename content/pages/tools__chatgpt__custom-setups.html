The layers available

  • Account-level instructions that apply to everything you do.
  • Per-setup instructions and reference files for one kind of job.
  • Shared setups, where a workspace plan makes them available to the team.

What to put where

Account level holds what's always true: who you are, the business, the tone, the never list. Per-setup holds the job: the format, the examples, the inclusions, the refusals. Mixing the two is the reason most people's instructions stop working — the general advice starts contradicting the specific job.

Shared setups and consistency

Where the team shares a setup, four people produce documents that look like one organisation. That's usually a bigger win than any individual's speed, and it's the argument that justifies a paid workspace plan.

Check what's shared before you share it
