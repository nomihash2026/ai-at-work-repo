The questions we get asked

I don't have time to sit in a course.

Correct, so there isn't one. We build one workflow with you on your own jobs until it runs, and that's the engagement.

I'm not a computer person.

The whole thing works by talking into your phone. If you can leave a voicemail you can do this.

Won't the quotes sound generic?

They sound like you, because we build them from the quotes you already send. That's most of the work.

Regulatory context

Relevant bodies and frameworks: State licensing authorities, Master Builders, MEA, NECA and equivalent industry associations. Licensing and sign-off obligations are unaffected by how a document was drafted. No claim is made about AI-generated compliance documentation being sufficient.

We name no CPD hour figures anywhere on this site. Requirements differ by body and by state, and an out-of-date number is worse than none.
