Where the line sits

Owner, tenant and buyer details are personal information. Almost none of the drafting work needs them, and the ones that do are named explicitly.

The boundary we teach

No representations about a property that aren't verified, no legal advice to a tenant or vendor.

The questions we get asked

My agents already use ChatGPT.

Most of them do, on the free tier, with no rule about it. That's the argument for training rather than against it — the gap is not access, it's method and boundaries.

AI listing copy all sounds the same.

It does when people accept the first draft. The session is mostly about getting output in your agency's voice and knowing which drafts to throw away.

Can we put sales and PM in one session?

You can, and half the room will be bored for half the time. The tasks barely overlap. Two shorter sessions work better.

Regulatory context

Relevant bodies and frameworks: State real estate regulators, REIA and state institutes. CPD requirements differ materially by state and territory. No national figure is claimed. Underquoting and misrepresentation rules apply to AI-drafted copy exactly as they do to human-drafted copy.

We name no CPD hour figures anywhere on this site. Requirements differ by body and by state, and an out-of-date number is worse than none.
