When the answer is neither

Two tests. First: if this output were wrong and nobody caught it, is the consequence a bad afternoon or a regulatory problem? Second: does doing this job require information that's on your never list? Either one means the tool isn't the answer, no matter how well it would perform.

Cost, honestly

Most small businesses end up paying for one tool properly rather than two, and which one depends on the shape of the work above. Where it genuinely warrants both, that's usually two or three people, not everyone.

The tier decision is the important one and it isn’t about price — tiers and what happens to your data.
