Get in touch

Tell us what the business does and what's eating the week. If we're the wrong fit we'll say so, and usually we can point you somewhere better.

Email

hello@ai-at-work.au — the fastest route, and read by a person.

What to include

  • What the business does and roughly how many people.
  • One or two jobs that are currently costing you time.
  • Whether anyone is already using AI, and whether there's a written rule.
  • Whether you're in one location or several.

Faster than an email

Run the assessment first. It takes about four minutes, it shows you the plan before asking for anything, and if you send it through it makes the first conversation much shorter and more accurate.

What happens next

  1. A short call to confirm what the actual problem is, which is sometimes not the one in the email.
  2. A written scope and figure, before any commitment.
  3. A date, or an honest no.

Please don't send client information

Not in an enquiry, not as an example. We'll ask for redacted documents if and when they become relevant.
