About

A training business for the admin and operational work that fills the week in Australian practices, clinics, firms, agencies, providers and field businesses.

What we do

We teach people to use AI on work they're already behind on. Not a survey of the field, not a tour of features — the documents, the reading and the chasing that actually consume a week, with the boundary drawn explicitly for the industry in the room.

The core skill set is the same everywhere. The examples, the deadlines and the compliance line are not, which is why the work is organised by industry even though the curriculum isn't.

How we work

  • Tool-neutral. Claude and ChatGPT, taught side by side. No reseller relationship or referral fee with either, and we'll tell you when the free tier is enough.
  • On your own documents. A generic exercise teaches a generic skill.
  • Boundary first. Where a wrong output is a regulated harm, that work stays with the qualified person and the training says so.
  • Setups built in the room. Notes to build later don't get built.

What we won't take on

Clinical medicine, financial advice, tax positions as distinct from tax administration, and anything requiring domain expertise we don't hold. We'd rather say no and stay useful than be confidently wrong in someone's regulated setting.

Where we work

Australia first, in person where the team is in one place and remotely where it isn't. Based in Sydney.

Who's behind it

AI at work is an independent training practice. The site and infrastructure are managed and maintained by DigiWolf.
