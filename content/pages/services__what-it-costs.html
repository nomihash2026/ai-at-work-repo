How we scope and price

No published price list yet, and we'd rather tell you why than put a fake range up.

Why there's no number on this page

Published prices are genuinely useful — they filter out badly-fitting enquiries before anyone wastes a call. The reason there isn't one here yet is that the engagement shapes are still being refined against real work, and a number published now would be wrong within a quarter.

When there is one it will go on this page, publicly, rather than being quoted case-by-case based on how the conversation is going.

What actually moves the number

FactorEffect
How many people in the roomThe main driver. A six-person practice and a forty-person provider are different engagements.
One site or severalMulti-site means either travel or a different delivery shape, and usually a policy that has to work without you in the room.
Policy work in front of trainingAdds an engagement rather than extending one. Common in legal, allied health and NDIS.
How much is built with youA workshop where setups get built in the room costs more than a talk and is worth considerably more.
Regulated complexityWhere the boundary work is substantial — clinical, legal, NDIS — the preparation is longer.

What we'll tell you on the first call

  • Whether we're the right fit, including when we're not.
  • Which engagement shape suits, and whether something should come before it.
  • A scope and a figure, in writing, before any commitment.
